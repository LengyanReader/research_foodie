## Answer (EN)

Nucleotides. A frameshift mutation is a deletion or insertion of one or more nucleotides that changes the reading frame of the base sequence (arXiv:SciQ:ctx).

核苷酸(nucleotides)。移码突变是指一个或多个核苷酸的缺失(deletion)或插入(insertion)，从而改变碱基序列的阅读框 (arXiv:SciQ:ctx)。
