## Answer (EN)
No. In a 1:2 case-control study of sex and age-matched subjects (n=114), carotid intima-media thickness (cIMT) was nearly identical between cases carrying mutations causing low HDL-C (10 different mutations in LCAT, ABCA1 and APOA1) and controls. The paper states these genetic HDL deficiency states "often do not correlate with premature CHD," consistent with cIMT not being increased (arXiv:SciQ:ctx).
