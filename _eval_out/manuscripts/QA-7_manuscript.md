## Answer (EN)

The Chinese data is the **FBFans** dataset — single-topic, Chinese, and highly unbalanced (arXiv:1611.03599). It contains posts from anti-nuclear-power Chinese Facebook fan groups (September 2013–August 2014). Its size:

- **Posts: 32,595 in total** — 26,754 training, 2,951 development, 2,890 testing (Table 1).
- **Unique users: 505,412**, comprising 2,496 authors, 505,137 likers, and 33,686 commenters.
- **Stance distribution** (Sup/Neu/Uns): 7,504 / 24,816 / 275 across all posts; training split is 7,097 / 19,412 / 245.
- An average of **161.1 users** engaged per post (max 23,297, min 1); an average of **3 comments per post** (max 1,092, min 0). About 20% of posts were stance-labeled.

## 中文速览

中文数据为 **FBFans** 数据集（反核能主题的中文 Facebook 粉丝专页，2013.09–2014.08），规模：帖子共 **32,595** 条（训练 26,754 / 开发 2,951 / 测试 2,890），立场分布 Sup/Neu/Uns = 7,504 / 24,816 / 275，严重不平衡。用户共 **505,412**（2,496 作者、505,137 点赞者、33,686 评论者）；平均每贴涉及 161.1 位用户、3 条评论。

## Source evidence

"The FBFans dataset contains data from anti-nuclear-power Chinese Facebook fan groups from September 2013 to August 2014, including posts and their author and liker IDs. There are a total of 2,496 authors, 505,137 likers, 33,686 commenters, and 505,412 unique users." (arXiv:1611.03599, §4.1)

"On average, 161.1 users were involved in one post. ... For comments, on average there were 3 comments per post." (arXiv:1611.03599, §4.1)
