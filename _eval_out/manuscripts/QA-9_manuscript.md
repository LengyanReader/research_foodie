## Answer (EN)

The paper evaluates question generation quality with six standard automatic metrics: **BLEU-1 (B1), BLEU-2 (B2), BLEU-3 (B3), BLEU-4 (B4)** (Papineni et al., 2002), **METEOR (MET)** (Denkowski and Lavie, 2014), and **ROUGE-L (R-L)** (Lin, 2004). All experiments are run on the SQuAD dataset using two data splits — the Zhou Split (Zhou et al., 2017) and the Du Split (Du et al., 2017). The evaluation script was released by Chen et al. (2015), and these are described as "all commonly-used metrics in question generation." (arXiv:1910.06036)

## 中文速览

评估使用六项标准自动指标：BLEU-1/2/3/4（Papineni et al., 2002）、METEOR（Denkowski and Lavie, 2014）和 ROUGE-L（Lin, 2004），并在 SQuAD 的两个划分（Zhou Split 与 Du Split）上进行，评测算子来自 Chen et al. (2015)。(arXiv:1910.06036)

## Source evidence

"We evaluate with all commonly-used metrics in question generation (Du et al., 2017): BLEU-1 (B1), BLEU-2 (B2), BLEU-3 (B3), BLEU-4 (B4) (Papineni et al., 2002), METEOR (MET) (Denkowski and Lavie, 2014) and ROUGE-L (R-L) (Lin, 2004). We use the evaluation script released by Chen et al. (2015)."
