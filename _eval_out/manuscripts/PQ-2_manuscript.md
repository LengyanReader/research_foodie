## Answer (EN)

The paper does not give a definitive answer on whether the Snellen E versus Landolt C acuity differs specifically in strabismus amblyopia. The stated study design covers "39 with amblyopia due to strabismus," but the abstract stops short of reporting an explicit result for that subgroup; it only states that such differences "cannot be excluded" (arXiv:SciQ:ctx).
