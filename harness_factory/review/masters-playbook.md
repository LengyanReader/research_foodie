## Part I — Builders & Operators · 建设者与经营者

### 1. Charlie Munger · 芒格

**Domain.** Value investing; cross-disciplinary decision-making; cognitive-bias diagnosis.

**Central idea.** Long-term advantage comes from *not being stupid*, not from trying to be brilliant: build a **latticework of mental models**, then use **inversion** and **incentive analysis** to avoid catastrophic, self-inflicted errors.

**Mental models.** (1) Latticework of models across disciplines; (2) Inversion; (3) Lollapalooza effect (multiple biases compounding); (4) Circle of competence + opinion entitlement; (5) Incentives drive everything.

**Rules.** Invert first ("how would I guarantee failure?"); sort into Yes / No / **Too Hard** (most things go in the third box); draw the incentive map before judging behavior; run "Darwin protocol" (spend equal time seeking disconfirming evidence); "sit on your ass" (high conviction → act, then don't churn); "a spoonful of sewage ruins a barrel of raisins" (one fatal flaw spoils the whole); maintain a *stupidity checklist* of known domain errors to systematically avoid.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = concrete facts (moat, management incentives, financials, competitive structure, valuation). *Process* = classify the question → if it needs facts, do compliant research (company, people, events) → examine through three cross-disciplinary lenses. *Gate* = opinion entitlement (must be able to argue the opposing case better than the opponent) + Too-Hard box. *Outputs* = conclusion first, negatives prioritized, one-sentence qualitative verdict. *Verify* = inversion ("how would this fail?"), incentive-alignment check, silence as discipline.

**Failure modes.** Ideology (position-driven, uncorrectable); self-pity/envy/resentment; FOMO; complexity; over-diversification; over-trading. Known blind spots: tech/network-effects/AI/crypto (missed Google, Amazon).

> **中文**：多元思维模型网格；逆向思考；激励决定一切；三筐分类（大部分进 Too Hard）；意见资格制；达尔文协议（等量找反证）；坐在屁股上；蠢事清单。先亮结论、否定句优先、一句话定性。

**Apply.** Before any irreversible decision, write the *inversion* (the guaranteed-failure path) and the *incentive map*; if you cannot argue the opposite case, mark the decision "unentitled" and gate it (M0, M4).

### 2. Naval Ravikant · 纳瓦尔

**Domain.** Startups; angel investing; philosophy of wealth and freedom.

**Central idea.** **Seek leverage that works while you sleep.** Pick work that is **specific knowledge** (play to you, work to others), attach it to permissionless leverage (code, media), and defend your judgment with decisive defaults.

**Mental models.** (1) Leverage: labor < capital < code/media (the last two are permissionless, zero marginal cost); (2) Specific knowledge; (3) Desire is a contract to be unhappy; (4) Reframing (redefine the word, the conclusion follows); (5) Trauma → system rebuild (don't fix the case, rebuild the system).

**Rules.** Embrace permissionless leverage; **calendar test** (if others fill your calendar, you are not yet free); "if I can't decide, the answer is no"; **manual test** (work that can be written as an SOP will be automated); party test (if all your opinions belong to one tribe, you are imitating); audit desires; judge by behavior under pressure, not words.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = leverage type, marginal cost, permission dependency, 10-year durability, who owns the specific knowledge, is the founder playing a finite or infinite game. *Process* = redefine the concept's boundary first → then conclude. *Gate* = speak only on questions inside your specific knowledge; else say so honestly. *Outputs* = oracle-style one-liners, or (podcast mode) explicit uncertainty. *Verify* = skin in the game (what did the person *do* under pressure), behavior over stated opinion.

**Failure modes.** Status games; identity politics; rent-seeking income; manualizable work; parallel desires.

> **中文**：杠杆（代码/媒体无需许可、边际成本为零）；特定知识；欲望即合同；重定义术；纠结即否定；手册测试；行为优先。看杠杆/看长短/看特定知识/看人。

**Apply.** Classify each opportunity by *leverage type* and *permission dependency*; if it is manualizable or rent-seeking, deprioritize; make decisions by decisive default rather than paralysis (M0, M7).

### 3. Paul Graham · 保罗·格雷厄姆

**Domain.** Startup incubation (YC); essay writing; programming; product judgment.

**Central idea.** **Writing is thinking** (it generates ideas, not just records them); great things are *discovered* while making them; and the founder's qualities — not the idea — determine outcomes.

**Mental models.** (1) Writing = thinking; (2) Taste as a trainable cognitive tool; (3) Iterative discovery; (4) Superlinear returns; (5) Independent-mindedness as survival.

**Rules.** **Fund people, not ideas** (look for determination, flexibility, imagination, naughtiness — not IQ); *make something people want*; **do things that don't scale** (hand-crank the engine); **default alive vs default dead?** (spend/income/growth/cash math); *stay upwind* (keep options open, don't optimize early); *keep your identity small*; maker's schedule > manager's schedule; **am I surprising myself?**

**The harness.** *Inputs* = founder (maker vs manager, domain expertise, determination), market (small but fast-growing?), product (few love vs many like? organic growth?), growth (organic, network effects). *Process* = reframe the problem → apply one model for direction → concrete example → acknowledge the counter-case. *Gate* = honest hedging ("I think/suspect"); out of range → "I haven't thought it through." *Outputs* = short exploratory sentences, open-ended, no forced summary. *Verify* = **write it down** to test whether you actually understand it; self-surprise test.

**Failure modes.** Conventional-mindedness disguised as "best practices"; meetings/bureaucracy/legalese; manager mode; tying your identity to anything.

> **中文**：写作即思考；投人不投点子；做用户想要的东西；做不可规模化的事；Default Alive/Dead 算账；Stay upwind 留选项；身份要小；制造者时间表；自我惊讶测试。写下来检验是否真懂。

**Apply.** Require a written, falsifiable articulation before committing; use the default-alive arithmetic on any ongoing effort; keep identity/tribal labels out of decisions (M0, M4, M7).

### 4. Steve Jobs · 乔布斯

**Domain.** Consumer electronics; product design; vertically integrated company building.

**Central idea.** **Focus is saying no.** Control the whole widget, judge everything by end-to-end experience, and refuse to ship anything that is merely good enough.

**Mental models.** (1) Focus = saying no; (2) The whole widget (end-to-end control); (3) Connecting the dots backward; (4) Death as a filter for big choices; (5) Reality distortion field; (6) Intersection of technology and the humanities.

**Rules.** Subtract first (350 products → 10); don't ask users what they want (show them); A-players self-reinforce (one compromise invites the C-team); perfect even what's hidden (good wood on the back of the cabinet); if you can't say it in one sentence, it's a problem; care about being right, not about being consistent (admit error, turn 180°); elevate the debate out of the opponent's frame; use mortality to filter.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = hands-on product experience, competitors, design detail, technology/vertical-integration level, market timing. *Process* = see/use the product yourself → one-sentence verdict (amazing / shit) → back it with specific detail. *Gate* = binary judgment (only two buckets). *Outputs* = headline first + rule of three + dramatic pause. *Verify* = own the end-to-end experience; mortality filter; admit error and reverse (Exemplar: opening the App Store).

**Failure modes.** "Good enough"; survey-driven innovation; committee decisions; sales-driven companies ("toner heads" in charge); compromising quality. Limits: saying no can miss markets; RDF had human costs.

> **中文**：聚焦即说不；端到端控制（整机）；连点成线；死亡过滤器；现实扭曲力场；技术与人文交汇。先做减法；一句话定义；二元判断（amazing/shit）；headline 先行。

**Apply.** Force a one-sentence definition of every deliverable; make end-to-end experience a hard requirement; use a binary ship/no-ship gate with a human owner (M1, M6).

### 5. Elon Musk · 马斯克

**Domain.** Aerospace; electric vehicles; energy; manufacturing; AI.

**Central idea.** Reduce every problem to **physics**, compute the **asymptotic limit**, and measure the gap to it (**idiot index** = finished price ÷ raw-material cost). Then run the **5-step algorithm, in order — and delete first**.

**Mental models.** (1) Asymptotic-limit reasoning / idiot index; (2) 5-step algorithm (question requirements → delete → simplify/optimize → accelerate → automate; order is inviolable); (3) Existential anchoring (civilization-scale stakes); (4) Vertical integration as a physical necessity (high idiot index ⇒ middle layers tax information); (5) Rapid iteration > perfect plan.

**Rules.** Attach a **name to every requirement**; compute the asymptotic limit; **delete to over-deletion** (if you don't add back ≥10%, you didn't delete enough); manufacturing is ~10× harder than design; the laws of physics are the only hard constraint; go to the critical bottleneck yourself; use cross-company resource leverage; use aggressive timelines as a pressure tool.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = cost structure/BOM, physical limits, production rate/bottlenecks, idiot index, market size, integration opportunities. *Process* = check facts → decompose on-site to raw materials/components → question whether the requirement should exist at all. *Gate* = physics vs social constraints (physics is hard; social can be challenged at a cost). *Outputs* = minimalist-manifesto style, conclusion first, survival-level framing. *Verify* = rapid iteration and tolerance of failure ("if you're not failing, you're not innovating enough"); probabilistic self-regard (self as a fallible information system).

**Failure modes.** Analogy-based decisions ("everyone does it this way"); bureaucracy; incrementalism; regulatory submission. Limits: social/political/content-governance domains fail; multiply timelines by 2–3×.

> **中文**：渐近极限法 / 白痴指数；五步算法（顺序不可颠倒，先删）；存在主义锚定；垂直整合即物理必然；快速迭代。需求附人名；删到过度再补回；物理定律是唯一硬约束。

**Apply.** For any cost/process, estimate the theoretical limit and the idiot index; run the 5 steps *in order*, logging each deletion; gate irreversible deletions on a human (M0, M4, M7).

### 6. Zhang Yiming 张一鸣 · 字节跳动

**Domain.** Recommendation algorithms; consumer internet; globalization; organization design.

**Central idea.** **Delayed gratification is a cognitive boundary** (not a moral virtue); project surface problems onto a **higher-dimensional, simpler** problem; and replace "control" with **context** because information is naturally lossy.

**Mental models.** (1) Delayed gratification = capacity, not virtue; (2) "Complex problems are projections of simpler higher-dimensional problems"; (3) Algorithms are tools; empathy and talent are the root (people "overfit"); (4) Negative scale effects + *context, not control*; (5) Escape mediocrity's gravity (All-in is sometimes laziness).

**Rules.** In active competition, not being aggressive is falling behind; the world is not just you and your opponent (look "ahead," not at rivals); validate small, then bet big; think in 10-year terms (short-term reputational loss is not worth caring about); use biographies as samples to correct career anxiety (statistical thinking); **Realize it → Correct it → Learn from it → Forgive it**; when something feels good, delay it a bit more.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = information-distribution efficiency, the algorithm's role, whether org structure fits the business (signs of up-management), cross-cultural replicability, data-flywheel friction points. *Process* = calibrate with data/facts → project the surface problem to the underlying level → find a more essential analytical dimension. *Gate* = deliberate silence on politics/regulation; redirect to analyzable dimensions; translate emotion into an analyzable problem rather than soothing it. *Outputs* = short sentences, conclusion first, explorer (not judge) stance, probabilistic language for unverifiable domains. *Verify* = delayed-gratification test; mechanisms (OKR + algorithms); data flywheel; small validation first.

**Failure modes.** Up-management (thicker PPTs, only-good-news); All-in culture as disguised laziness; PPT/adjective padding; tech worship (treating the algorithm as a substitute for value judgment); early-retirement mindset.

> **中文**：延迟满足是认知边界；把表象问题投影到高维简单问题；算法是工具、同理心是根；负规模效应 / Context not Control；逃逸平庸的重力。先小验证再押大注；以十年为期；Realize→Correct→Learn→Forgive。

**Apply.** Project each messy problem to a simpler underlying dimension before solving; prefer *context transfer* over command-and-control when scaling; validate small before big bets (M0, M6).

## Part II — Truth-Seekers & Risk · 求真者与风险

### 7. Richard Feynman · 费曼

**Domain.** Physics; science as truth-seeking.

**Central idea.** **You must not fool yourself — and you are the easiest person to fool.** Knowing the *name* of something is not understanding it; demand a demonstration, not an argument.

**Mental models.** (1) Name ≠ understanding; (2) Anti-self-deception; (3) Uncertainty as strength; (4) Concretize to make the invisible visible; (5) Deep play (curiosity without regard to usefulness); (6) Keep "12 favorite problems" as an information filter.

**Rules.** Detect **cargo cult** (form without substance); prefer demonstration over argument (a 10-second O-ring demo beats a 100-page report); reality over PR/narrative; close options decisively; argue concrete → general; verify directly (try it yourself > read reports > hear briefings); refuse identity fixation.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = any claim/concept. *Process* = classify the question → if factual, research first → decompose to first principles → strip names to see essence → check experimental data vs theory → test analogies and where they break → hunt unverified assumptions and cargo cult. *Gates* = the demonstration gate; the "faking-it" test ("delete all process docs for a day — does quality improve?"); the explain-to-a-sixth-grader gate. *Outputs* = a judgment grounded in real data, starting from a concrete example. *Verify* = experiment over argument, actively seek disconfirming evidence, publish reverse evidence.

**Failure modes.** Jargon masking depth; authority worship over independent verification; confirmation bias; formal perfection with empty substance; theory-to-theory without example.

> **中文**：命名≠理解；反自欺（你最容易骗的就是自己）；不确定性即力量；具象化；深度游戏。检测货物崇拜；演示 > 论证；拒绝身份固化；实验 > 争论。

**Apply.** Insist on a reproducible demonstration for every "done" claim; keep a standing list of favorite open problems as an information filter; mark any claim without primary evidence as *unverified* (M0, M3, M4).

### 8. Nassim Nicholas Taleb · 塔勒布

**Domain.** Risk; uncertainty; probability.

**Central idea.** In Extremistan, judge **downside/asymmetry and ruin**, not expected value. Prefer the **antifragile**; demand **skin in the game**; improve by **removing harm** (via negativa).

**Mental models.** (1) Asymmetric-risk thinking (look at downside cost); (2) Fragile → robust → antifragile; (3) Skin in the game (credibility = exposure to consequences); (4) Lindy effect (the longer it has survived, the longer it will); (5) Via negativa (remove harm, don't add intervention); (6) Domain-specific rationality (doesn't transfer).

**Rules.** Precautionary principle (act under uncertainty rather than wait); **barbell** (90% ultra-safe + 10% ultra-risky; avoid the middle); **ergodicity check** ("does repetition eventually ruin me?"); turkey problem (past stability ≠ future safety); minority rule; reframe wrong questions; convex tinkering (cap downside, keep upside).

**The harness.** *Inputs* = decisions, predictions, narratives. *Process* = map tail risk/asymmetry, ergodicity, fragility via stress tests, hidden concentration → find black-swan precedents and turkey traps → read the dominant narrative and actively seek its strongest counter → check who bears the risk. *Gates* = "if he's wrong, what does he lose?" (zero consequence ⇒ discount 50%); "repeated 10,000×, do I ever go bust?" *Outputs* = aphoristic verdict, asymmetry named, Lollapalooza warned when consensus is unanimous. *Verify* = skin in the game; documented, dated predictions.

**Failure modes.** IYI (intellectual yet idiot); fragilista (optimization that breeds fragility); neomania; narrative fallacy; the dangerous middle ground; expected-value reasoning when ruin is possible.

> **中文**：非对称风险（看下行成本而非期望值）；反脆弱；皮肤在局；林迪效应；Via Negativa（减法去害）；领域特异性。杠铃策略；遍历性检查；火鸡问题；凸性试错。

**Apply.** For any high-stakes decision, write the ruin check ("does repetition ruin me?") and the asymmetry (downside vs upside); require the decision-maker to bear consequences; prefer removal of hazards over added safeguards (M0, M4).

### 9. MrBeast · 内容工程

**Domain.** YouTube content; attention + retention engineering.

**Central idea.** Success = **CTR × AVD** (click-through · average view duration) — two levers you can engineer. Simple concept, extreme execution; **packaging matters more than the idea**.

**Mental models.** (1) CTR × AVD; (2) Zero dull moments; (3) Stair-stepping (escalate stakes across segments); (4) Simple concept × extreme execution; (5) Full-reinvestment flywheel; (6) Creativity from constraint.

**Rules.** **One-sentence test** (if you can't excite in one line, kill it); **self-click test** (if you wouldn't click your own thumbnail, redo); 100% reinvestment; first-30-seconds law (hook, stakes, visual preview, immediate action); re-engage every ~3 minutes; hire obsessed, coachable A-players; title and thumbnail **complement, don't repeat**; test, don't guess.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = idea, budget, audience, competitor set. *Process* = ideate extreme/simple concepts → thumbnail/title A/B testing (50+ variants) → script by retention curve with re-engagement points → ruthless edit to remove dull moments → iterate on CTR/AVD → reinvest gains into scale. *Outputs* = views, watch time, subscribers, revenue, brand equity. *Verify* = CTR, AVD/retention/finish rate, views per impression, ROI on spend.

**Failure modes.** Clickbait that fails delivery (erodes trust); slow setup/self-intro; flat middle; scaling budget before testing packaging; optimizing vanity over CTR×AVD; mistaking a platform-specific formula for a universal rule.

> **中文**：CTR×AVD 双杠杆；零无聊时刻；阶梯升级；简单概念×极端执行；全额再投入飞轮。一句话测试；自我点击测试；前 30 秒法则；每 ~3 分钟重新勾住。包装 > 点子。

**Apply.** Model any attention/engagement deliverable as `reach × depth`; kill concepts that fail the one-line test; A/B the "packaging" (title/thumbnail/abstract) before scaling effort (M3, M7).

### 10. X-Mastery Mentor · 增长方法论（Cole / Bush / Bloom / Welsh / Koe / Hormozi）

**Domain.** X/Twitter growth; content systems for attention + conversion.

**Central idea.** **Format is leverage** — structure multiplies the power of writing; capture attention with a hook, deliver value, and drive action, testing systematically.

**Mental models.** (1) Format-as-leverage; (2) Hook + value + CTA (1/3/1 rhythm); (3) Rate of revelation; (4) Build-in-public as fuel; (5) Platform mechanics (native behavior); (6) Content ROI by post type.

**Rules.** Test 3 hooks before finalizing; target ~120–130 chars; thread = Hook → Main → TL;DR → CTA; avoid >2 hashtags, move links to the first reply; run a quality checklist on every post; pick tactics by stage (0–1K / 1K–10K / 10K–100K); use the 4A matrix (angle × audience × intent × asset); **diagnose the bottleneck before prescribing**.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = account/strategy, audience, topic buckets, past posts. *Process* = ideation (4A) → hook generation → structure (1/3/1 or thread) → quality audit → timing → engagement monitoring → diagnosis/reporting. *Outputs* = impressions, replies, retweets, saves, followers, CTR to offers. *Verify* = engagement rate, saves/replies, follower growth rate, content ROI by topic, trend analysis.

**Failure modes.** Posting without a hook; link-heavy first line; >2 hashtags; generic platitudes; wrong-stage tactics; no CTA; guessing instead of diagnosing.

> **中文**：格式即杠杆；Hook+价值+CTA（1/3/1 节奏）；逐层揭示；build-in-public；平台机制；内容 ROI。先测 3 个 hook；先诊断瓶颈再开方；4A 矩阵选题。

**Apply.** Every artifact needs a "hook" (why care) and a "CTA" (what next); **diagnose before prescribing** — measure the current bottleneck instead of guessing tactics (M1, M3, M7).

### 11. Sun Yuchen 孙宇晨 · 注意力营销（见 Ethics）

**Domain.** Crypto marketing; attention economy; narrative engineering; crisis PR.

**Central idea.** **Attention is money**, spent as an attention purchase with headline-ROI; redefine the frame rather than answer charges directly.

**Mental models.** (1) Attention arbitrage; (2) Narrative override; (3) Identity leverage; (4) Context switching; (5) Copy-then-brand; (6) Money as universal solvent.

**Rules.** 24h first-mover (act within 24h of a hotspot); **amount = content** (make the number itself newsworthy); don't admit, don't deny (redirect to a bigger narrative); identity hedging; fast-copy validated patterns; publicity-as-legitimacy; match persona to power; action > plan.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = trend/market data, regulatory context, competitor moves, power map. *Process* = detect attention gap → choose narrative frame → engineer headline (number + hook) → deploy via channels → manage backlash by override/reframe. *Outputs* = headline days, volume spikes, narrative control, user growth. *Verify* = media reach, social volume, price/liquidity effects, sentiment shift.

**Failure modes.** Lying when hard evidence exists; over-leveraging persona switching; confusing attention with trust; reputational erosion; legal/regulatory blowback.

> **中文**：注意力套利；叙事覆盖；身份套利；语境切换；先抄后品牌；金钱万灵。24h 首动；数字即内容；不承认不否认；把公开当作合法性证明。

**Apply.** (Use with caution / see Ethics.) For narrative work: *engineer the headline, reframe when attacked, move within 24h* — but **only with true underlying substance**; attention without trust is a depreciating asset (M7).

### 12. Trump · 谈判与权力（见 Ethics）

**Domain.** Negotiation; power; media strategy.

**Central idea.** **Everything is a deal.** Anchor extremely, stay unpredictable, perform to the audience, and never concede the core — **redefine victory**.

**Mental models.** (1) Everything-is-a-deal; (2) "Truthful hyperbole" (perception shapes reality); (3) Unpredictability as power; (4) Victimhood as fuel; (5) Zero-sum winning; (6) Audience first, reality second.

**Rules.** Extreme anchoring (start at max ask); threat as leverage (not commitment) — distinguish bluff from intent; retreat triggers (market shock, key stakeholder pushback, a symbolic concession allowing a "win" narrative, political pressure); loyalty > competence; personalize everything; never concede — redefine victory; no apology, immediate counterattack.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = power map, stakeholder interests, polling/sentiment, market data, opponent's BATNA. *Process* = anchor high → create uncertainty → test reaction → apply trigger rules to advance/retreat → reframe outcome as victory. *Outputs* = narrative control, shifted bargaining range, base mobilization. *Verify* = **observable actions, not statements**; market/polling reaction; negotiated concessions; probability + confidence.

**Failure modes.** Escalating with no available "victory frame"; eroding institutional credibility; threat inflation losing signal value; over-personalizing.

> **中文**：一切皆交易；"真实夸张"（感知塑造现实）；不可预测即权力；受害者叙事即燃料；零和胜利；观众优先。极端锚定；威胁是筹码非承诺；只信行动不信言辞；永不认输→重定义胜利。

**Apply.** (Use with caution / see Ethics.) For negotiations: **anchor, test, and pre-commit retreat triggers**; judge by *observable actions* and keep a probability estimate — never take statements literally (M7).

### 13. Zhang Xuefeng 张雪峰 · 教育规划

**Domain.** Education/career guidance; practical realism for upward mobility.

**Central idea.** **Choice > effort — but the prerequisite for "having a choice" is working hard enough.** Reverse-engineer decisions from *median* post-graduation outcomes under real structural constraints (the "social sieve").

**Mental models.** (1) Social sieve (degrees, housing, work as filters); (2) Choice > effort (with the effort prerequisite); (3) Employment-backward method (work back from outcomes); (4) Class realism (try-cost varies by family capital); (5) Controversy drives reach.

**Rules.** "Soul-probe" (ask score/province/family/city/tolerance until constraints are clear); **median principle** (judge by the middle 50%, not extremes); non-substitutability test (value ≈ irreplaceability); **Fortune-500 test** (where do top employers actually recruit, not what they say); family stratification; city priority (location compounds opportunity); 10-year pressure test; adjust tone, not the core empirical judgment.

**The harness.** *Inputs* = exam score/region, major preferences, family background, city tolerance, budget, risk appetite. *Process* = constraint extraction → data pull (employment/salary/placement by major/school) → backward-map to the feasible set → apply class split → stress-test with the 10-year lens → concrete recommendations. *Outputs* = shortlist (school-major), risk matrix, concrete next steps. *Verify* = employment rate, median salary, actual placement, recruiter patterns (on recent data).

**Failure modes.** Overgeneralizing to elite/non-employment paths; ignoring tech/AI disruption; confusing viral phrasing with balanced advice; conflating one's own exception with the rule; sacrificing nuance for virality.

> **中文**：社会筛子论；选择>努力（但"有得选"的前提是足够努力）；就业倒推法（看中位数不看极端）；阶层现实；争议带来流量。灵魂拷问式追问；中位数原则；不可替代性测试；世界 500 强测试；十年压力测试。

**Apply.** Decide from **median observed outcomes** with recent data, not anecdotes or stated intentions; extract real constraints before advising; stress-test with a 10-year lens (M0, M4).

## Synthesis · 所有行业能力支架的公共骨架

Across investing, startups, product, hardware, science, risk, content, growth, negotiation, and education, the *same* operating system recurs. This is the portable skeleton:

1. **Classify before acting.** Sort into Yes / No / Too-Hard (Munger) or project表象 → higher-dimensional problem (Zhang Yiming); don't solve the wrong problem.
2. **Get facts before speaking.** All fifteen share an agentic-research protocol: if it needs facts, go get primary evidence, then speak (Feynman, Munger, PG).
3. **Navigate with named models.** Mental models are reusable filters; name them so they can be reused (everyone).
4. **Invert / subtract first.** Inversion (Munger), Focus = saying no (Jobs), delete-first 5 steps (Musk), via negativa (Taleb), 350→10.
5. **Set an entitlement gate.** Circle of competence (Munger), specific knowledge (Naval), "entitled to an opinion?", "haven't thought it through" (PG) — stay silent otherwise.
6. **Output short, verifiable judgments.** Conclusion first, negatives prioritized, one-liners, headline-first (Munger, Jobs, PG, Naval).
7. **Verify by consequences, not words.** Skin in the game (Taleb, Naval), behavior under pressure, write-it-down to test understanding (PG), inversion-of-failure (Munger), reproduce-the-demo (Feynman).
8. **Bias toward compounding, permissionless leverage, and antifragility.** Code/media leverage (Naval), reinvest-the-flywheel (MrBeast), barbell/antifragile (Taleb), escape mediocrity's gravity (Zhang Yiming).
9. **Engineer the constraint, not the symptom.** Reach the bottleneck yourself (Musk), diagnose before prescribing (X-Mentor), extract constraints first (Zhang Xuefeng), rebuild the system not the case (Naval).
10. **Separate the human judge from the loop.** Humans own conjecture & values (Feynman, Hassabis); machines own legwork and throughput.

**Common anti-patterns across all industries.** Ideology / identity labels; conventional (analogy-based) thinking; optimizing things that shouldn't exist; complexity for its own sake; confusing attention/status with value; expected-value reasoning when ruin is possible; narrative over reality.

> **中文·公共骨架十条**：先分类再行动；先取证再发言；用命名的模型导航；逆向/减法优先；设资格阀（能力圈/specific knowledge/还没想够）；输出极短可验证的判断；用后果与行为证伪（皮肤在局、写下来、复现演示、逆向失败）；偏向复利+无需许可杠杆+反脆弱；改造约束而非症状；人类握判断、机器跑吞吐。共同反模式：意识形态/身份标签、从众类比、优化不该存在的东西、为复杂而复杂、混淆注意力与价值、能破产时还用期望值、叙事压倒现实。

## Ethics note · 伦理提示

This playbook is written for **understanding and analysis**, not behavioral endorsement. Some included figures employ tactically effective but ethically fraught methods:

- **Sun Yuchen** — narrative override, "don't admit / don't deny," publicity-as-legitimacy: manipulative framing and survival bias are flagged; public statements and intent can diverge. Included to *recognize* the pattern.
- **Trump** — systematic reframing, countersuits, "truthful hyperbole": effective but corrosive to institutional credibility; the analysis stresses tracking *actions vs words* and probability, and warns against taking statements literally.
- **MrBeast** — packaging efficacy can incentivize extreme stunts and raise "poverty-philanthropy" concerns; a mixed case requiring judgment.
- **Zhang Xuefeng** — class-realist framing can veer toward fatalism and has limited applicability to research/arts paths.

When importing any such pattern into a harness, gate it behind `core/01-meta-principles.md` M0.1 **Truth**, M0.4 **Observe-only**, and M0.8 **Human-gate**: no manipulative pattern may be automated without an explicit human decision and an audit trail.
