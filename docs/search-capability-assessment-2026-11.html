3 · 探针实测与两个缺陷 / The probe and the two defects it caught

The standing battery passes 44/44 — but its sentences are multi-signal. Real users write short ones. Two minimal one-signal probes failed before P74:

multi-word signals (containing a space) weight 2 so "comparative politics" outvotes "politics". Chinese has no spaces: all 149 two-character zh signals — and everything else — scored 1. Consequence: 从认识论角度重构论证 scored 认识论(philosophy, 1) vs 重构(software-engineering, 1), the tie broke by registry order, and the task routed to

(认识论, 类型注解, 民族志 — 68 signals) now scores 2, mirroring the English multi-word rule. The zh signal-length distribution motivated the threshold: 2-char words are often generic (重构, 模型, 设计), 3+-char compounds are almost always multi-morpheme discipline terms.

so 构建知识图谱并注明来源 fell to general (the capability still fired via the knowledge_base artifact, but the domain — and therefore the domain playbook — was wrong). Fix: signals added to digital-humanities, consistent with the battery's existing intent (构建方志人物知识图谱… → digital-humanities) and with knowledge-management's distinct ownership of 知识库. Single ownership preserved (the one-owner distinctiveness guard).

4 · 同轮修复的注册表契约缺陷 / Same-round fix: the registry contract conflict

Probing also surfaced a pre-existing contradiction among three validators, exposed by the agent-eval-reliability determinism family (P67): validate_battery demanded a trial_projects binding; the metabolism test demanded paired (both-arm) receipts; and a determinism test has no bare arm by design — the working tree carried a half-finished edit that "resolved" this by inserting a fabricated A-bare: 0.0 (an M0.4 violation: a measurement that never happened). P74 resolves it honestly:

  • validate_battery: a done family may declare control_arm: "none"; the numeric

A-bare check is waived (the B-harness arm stays numeric). No number is invented.

  • reconcile: a control_arm=none binding is receipted by any task_outcome

(receipted_projects), not only both-arm pairs — still trace-verified, just one-armed.

  • Synthetic regression added (test_control_arm_none_receipted_by_one_arm); suite 549 green.

6 · 结论 / Verdict

The pipeline is a strong router (en/zh, 34 domains, 46-case battery, and after P74 a scoring rule that treats Chinese terms with the same specificity logic as English multi-word phrases) and an honestly-scoped retriever (single-hop by design, agent owns multi-hop). The two defects the probe caught were real user-facing misroutes, both fixed at root rather than by symptom patching. Remaining gaps are named, scored, and carry concrete recommendations (R1 confidence margin; R2 multi-hop derive mode) rather than vague aspirations.
