Executive summary. This review covers late October to early November 2026, drawing on 3,336 intelligence items (418 from arXiv) and three full-read primary sources (R279 ThinkingBox · R280 QueenBee · R281 LegoSR). Three core findings: (1) the harness skeleton has converged; differentiation now lives in verification gates and provenance — confirmed independently by our eight-family paired trials (bare mean 0.27 vs harness 0.99) and ThinkingBox's industrial-scale data (77.61% of silently failing runs carry wrong database state); (2) evaluation standards are shifting from pass@1 to consistency — ThinkingBox shows the two diverge sharply (93.89% vs 13.41%), and the metric you choose determines the conclusion you get; (3) governance infrastructure is consolidating — Agent Skills donated to the Linux Foundation's AAIF (70+ platforms, 1M skills, 280M installs), with Open Responses / OpenEnv / OpenSandbox launched in the same batch. 4 · 新兴方向 · Emerging directions

中文

  • 约束解码 / 结构化输出：Outlines、Instructor（Apache-2.0）——「让模型只在合法语法里说话」的工程化，是 schema-valid 输出契约的低成本路径。
  • 时序知识图谱记忆：Graphiti（Apache-2.0）——强调随时间演化的实体关系，相对静态向量库是有意义的差异化。
  • 沙箱执行：E2B、OpenEnv（与 R279 同源）——「有状态副作用可判分」正在变成通用底座。
  • 本地推理：vLLM（Apache-2.0，活跃）——自托管与成本敏感场景的现实选择。
  • 护栏与网关：NeMo Guardrails、LiteLLM——许可证 NOASSERTION，未过入表门，登记待核。

English

  • Constrained decoding / structured output: Outlines, Instructor (Apache-2.0) — engineering "the model speaks only within legal grammar" as a low-cost path to schema-valid output contracts.
  • Temporal knowledge-graph memory: Graphiti (Apache-2.0) — entity relationships that evolve over time, a meaningful differentiation from static vector stores.
  • Sandboxed execution: E2B, OpenEnv (same lineage as R279) — "stateful side effects are gradable" is becoming general-purpose infrastructure.
  • Local inference: vLLM (Apache-2.0, active) — the realistic choice for self-hosted, cost-sensitive scenarios.
  • Guardrails and gateway: NeMo Guardrails, LiteLLM — license NOASSERTION, intake gate not yet passed.

5 · 分析与预测 · Analytical projections

中文

以下四条预测从上述证据逻辑推出——每条注明支撑证据、置信度与反转条件。它们不是猜测，是从已观察事实中提取的可检验推论。

支撑证据：R279 数据显示 pass@1 与 20/20 一致性尖锐分化；R269 ABC 检查单将任务有效性与结果有效性列为可检验主张；本库 M3.19 落地了同一纪律。 置信度：高（三个独立来源收敛）。 反转条件：出现一个被广泛采纳的一致性基准标准且与 pass@1 不矛盾。

支撑证据：R278 AAIF 四项目含 OpenSandbox（阿里）与 OpenEnv（HF+Meta）；R279 ThinkingBox 已经使用 OpenEnv 适配器；三个独立来源在同一个接口上收敛。 置信度：中高（产品与标准同步收敛，但实际部署尚未大规模验证）。 反转条件：OpenEnv 被更广泛的行业接口标准取代，或 ThinkingBox 的开源采用率显著低于预期。

支撑证据：R279 数据显示 pass@1 与 20/20 分化的根因是「四分之三失败为工具处理」——即记忆检索的工具调用稳定性而非记忆容量是瓶颈。P52 记录的 Mem0「平台数字 vs OSS 数字」分歧进一步说明：记忆系统的评估需要区分「最佳情况」与「可靠情况」。 置信度：中（证据来自两个来源，但记忆系统本身的演化方向仍受多种因素影响）。 反转条件：出现一种记忆架构在一致性与容量之间实现了真正的权衡突破。

支撑证据：R276 记录 Agent Skills 捐入 AAIF（70+ 平台、1M 技能、2.8 亿安装），公告明确提出下一步是「版本化发布、正式提案流程、安全指引」——即认证轨道；同时社区技能持续增长（superpowers 一类方法论框架跨六个 agent 平台运行）。两条轨道服务的需求不同（企业合规 vs 快速迭代）。 置信度：中（基于治理结构与社区趋势的推理，非实测）。 反转条件：AAIF 认证流程被社区广泛抵制，或社区技能的安全事件导致全面认证需求。

English

Four projections logically follow from the evidence above. Each cites its supporting evidence, confidence level, and reversal condition.

Evidence: R279 shows pass@1 and 20/20-consistency diverge sharply; R269 (ABC) lists task validity and outcome validity as checkable claims; this library's M3.19 codifies the same discipline. Three independent sources converge. Confidence: high. Reversal: a widely adopted consistency benchmark standard emerges that does not conflict with pass@1.

Evidence: R278 AAIF includes OpenSandbox (Alibaba) and OpenEnv (HF+Meta); R279 ThinkingBox already uses the OpenEnv adapter; three independent sources converge on the same interface. Confidence: medium-high (product and standard converging, but large-scale deployment not yet demonstrated). Reversal: a broader industry interface standard displaces OpenEnv, or ThinkingBox's open-source adoption falls significantly short.

Evidence: R279 attributes the pass@1/consistency divergence to tool-handling failures (79.9%), not reasoning — meaning retrieval stability, not memory capacity, is the bottleneck. P52's Mem0 "platform vs OSS" divergence further shows that memory evaluation must distinguish "best case" from "reliable case." Confidence: medium (two-source evidence; memory-system evolution influenced by many factors). Reversal: a memory architecture achieves a genuine breakthrough in the consistency-capacity trade-off.

Evidence: R276 documents Agent Skills donated to AAIF (70+ platforms, 1M skills, 280M installs), with next steps explicitly including "versioned releases, formal proposal process, security guidance" — the certified track. Meanwhile community skills continue growing (superpowers runs across six agent platforms). The two tracks serve different needs (enterprise compliance vs rapid iteration). Confidence: medium (reasoning from governance structure and community trends, not direct measurement). Reversal: broad community resistance to AAIF certification, or a major community-skill security incident forcing universal certification.
